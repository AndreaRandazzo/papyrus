<p>Papyrus keeps your important documents in one tidy place on your phone: ID cards, passports, insurance policies, medical tests, contracts, receipts and warranties.</p>

<p><b>Private by design.</b> Everything stays on your device. No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking. Papyrus has no internet access and can't read your storage: it only sees the files you pick.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Add PDFs, Word files and photos; they are copied safely into the app</li>
<li>Scan paper documents with the camera into a multi-page PDF, cropped to the paper's corners</li>
<li>Share files to Papyrus from any app</li>
<li>Organize with categories, each with its own icon, and browse them as folders</li>
<li>Optional app lock with your fingerprint, face or phone PIN</li>
<li>Notes and expiry dates for every document</li>
<li>Expiring tab: see what has expired or expires in the next 30 days</li>
<li>Optional reminders 30 and 7 days before a document expires, and on the day</li>
<li>Search inside your documents: Papyrus reads the text of PDFs, scans and photos on the phone (Italian and English), nothing is sent anywhere</li>
<li>Fast search across titles, notes, categories and that text (ignores accents)</li>
<li>Filter by type and date added, sort by date, title or expiry in either direction</li>
<li>Previews of PDF pages and photos, list, grid or folders view</li>
<li>Open and share documents with other apps</li>
<li>Backup and restore your whole library as a single file</li>
<li>Light and dark theme, English and Italian</li>
</ul>

<p><b>About backups:</b> a backup is a normal ZIP file saved where you choose. It is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.</p>
